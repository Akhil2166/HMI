Abstract

Examples of the present disclosure describe systems and methods for performing cryptographic operations in isolated sets. In an example, a user may have associated user resources within the isolated set, which may be associated with a cryptographic key. Other users may access the user key from a known location to perform one or more encryption operations manually or automatically. In another example, the key may be generated when a group session is initiated. The key may be encrypted using the public key of each participant and provided to each participant. Each participant may then use the cryptographic key during the conversation. The new participant may receive authorization to join the conversation from the existing participant, wherein the public key of the new participant may be used to decrypt and re-encrypt the cryptographic key of the existing participant. The new participant may then use the re-encrypted key to participate in the conversation.

Cryptographic operations in isolated collections
Technical Field
The present invention relates generally to a solution for performing cryptographic operations in isolated sets.
Background
Cryptography can provide a variety of authentication and data protection tools. As an example, the public key of an asymmetric key pair may be used to encrypt data and verify a cryptographic signature, while the private key of an asymmetric key pair may be used to decrypt data and generate a cryptographic signature. As a result, both parties may securely communicate using multiple asymmetric key pairs (e.g., one key pair for each party), verify the source of the received data, and verify the identity of the other party. Conventionally, establishing such cryptographic systems requires at least one of a variety of key exchange protocols or mechanisms to ensure that users within the cryptographic system have access to the necessary keys (e.g., encryption and signature verification, among others) for performing such cryptographic operations.
With respect to these and other general considerations, aspects disclosed herein have been made. Moreover, while relatively specific problems may be discussed, it should be appreciated that these embodiments should not be limited to addressing the specific problems identified in the background or elsewhere in this disclosure.
Disclosure of Invention
Examples of the present disclosure describe systems and methods for performing cryptographic operations in isolated sets. In an example, a user may be associated with a user resource within the isolated collection. The user resource may be associated with one or more cryptographic keys, which may be automatically generated or received from the user. In some examples, providing the cryptographic key may be a prerequisite to gaining access to the isolated set. In one example, a user may encrypt a resource by accessing a public key associated with a recipient stored by an isolated collection. In another example, the recipient may verify the cryptographic signature using a public key associated with the sender stored by the isolated collection. As a result, such encryption operations may occur automatically because the necessary cryptographic keys may be accessed from a known location by other users of the isolated collection.
In another example, the cryptographic key may be generated upon initiation of the dialog session. Messages sent during a conversation session may be encrypted using a cryptographic key and stored in a quarantined collection. In some examples, the message may also be signed as described above. The cryptographic key may be encrypted using the public key of each session participant and provided to each session participant. When a new session participant wishes to gain access to a conversation session, authorization may be requested from and provided by an existing session participant. Authorization may cause the encrypted cryptographic key of the existing session participant to be decrypted, which may then be re-encrypted using the public key of the new session participant. The re-encrypted cryptographic key may then be provided to the new session participant, and the new session participant may then use it to access and participate in the conversation session.
